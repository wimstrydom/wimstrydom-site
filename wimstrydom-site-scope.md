# wimstrydom.com — Site Scope & Roadmap

A personal website structured around five sections, each representing a different mode of engagement: who I am, what I imagine, what I investigate, what I build, and what I savour.

---

## 1. Definitions
*Who I am, what I believe, my experiences and how to reach me.*

### Coordinates
A personal introduction. Where I'm from, what I do, what drives me. Why this site exists.

### Philosophy
My stances on core questions. Declarative positions, considered, and revisable.

### Curriculum Vitae
A professional record of my work history, roles, and experience.

### Contact
A simple contact form for reaching me directly.

---

## 2. Narratives
*The things I imagine and feel — fiction, poetry, and the stories within the lines.*

### Short Stories
Original fiction. Each story published as its own page.

### Poems
Original poetry. Each poem published as its own page.

---

## 3. Explorations
*Essays paired with the maths that inspired them. Each exploration begins as a question and ends with something you can use (or at least play with if not use).*

### Spin Station *(live)*
An investigation into the physics of a transit system aboard a large rotating space station. Accompanied by an interactive dashboard for exploring how felt gravity changes in magnitude and direction across a journey on board a configurably fictional space station.

### The Fibonacci Roulette *(coming soon)*
An exploration of the statistics behind playing roulette with the Fibonacci method, and the broader implications of gambling as a system. Accompanied by a probability calculator.

### The Value of an Avios Point *(coming soon)*
A personal and relative valuation of Avios points, what they're actually worth across different redemption scenarios. Accompanied by a calculator for estimating your own personalised value (of an Avios point, not of you, I'm not an actuary).

### The Advent of Hyper-Personalised Software *(coming soon)*
An examination of what software looks like when it adapts completely to the individual — and what that means for how we build and use tools. Accompanied by a few illustrations of a likely future.

---

## 4. Instruments
*Things I've built. Each instrument is a working tool, not a prototype.*

### Personal Finance Dashboard
An AI-powered personal finance app. Automatically categorises transactions, tracks spending relative to budget, and surfaces patterns over time.

### Travel & Leave Planner
A planning tool for coordinating travel and leave. Designed to make the logistics of time off easier to manage.

### Family Recipe Translator
An app for capturing and translating family recipes, preserving and celebrating them across languages and generations.

---

## 5. Salivations
*Food and drink worth remembering: Things I've made and places I've been.*

### Creations

Dishes and cocktails worth recording. Each recipe is a thing worth making again.

### Experiences
Encounters with places worth writing about. Not ratings — impressions.

---

## Homepage
*The homepage is not a section. It is a map.*

The homepage orients a first-time visitor and rewards a returning one. It creates curiosity. Structurally it should:

- Introduce me and the site briefly (a sentence or two, not a paragraph)
- Surface the five sections with enough description to be intriguing
- Feel like a letter, not a directory
