# Agony of possibility

I barrel down the road

The tank is emptying 

And I shudder



At the start I could venture

Wherever I resolved

With shoulders only my own



My pursuit consumes

I get motion sick and

Curse my lack of progress



My ends have no limit

I see every destination

But commit to none



Fixed despite the chase

Why won't the mountains

Get any closer?



Dreams scrape nightmares

Hope lies between them 

And slowly I lose it



Possibility collapses

Into plausibility 

And so do I
